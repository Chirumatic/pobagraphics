Who We Are

Turning Ideas Into Extraordinary Success

Poba Graphics Limited is a creative graphics design, printing, and corporate branding company established in 2015 by a team of professional designers who saw the need to fill significant gaps in the graphics and printing industry.

With 10 years of hands-on experience in digital media and modern printing technology, we are fully equipped to tackle any media challenge. Our strength lies in blending deep knowledge of both local and international market trends with innovative approaches to communication.

This balanced combination, coupled with our proven expertise, enables Poba Graphics to deliver high-quality solutions, effectively address clients' media needs, and consistently exceed expectations.

How We Work

Our Designing Process

01
Research

Depending on the scope of the project, we include as much valuable information as possible to build a better understanding of the content for a better, more original design.

02
Concept

We choose what will work best to fulfil the client's wishes, whittling choices down to one standout concept — original, unique, and different from anything available on the market.

03
Design

Our creative team implements best practices in colour, typography, hierarchy of information, and positive and negative space — using the right software and tools efficiently.

04
Prototype

We develop drafts and physical samples representing the client's idea. This stage is critical in the approval process as it visualises the actual refined product.

05
Production

After all approvals, artwork is forwarded to our specialised personnel in Digital, Press, Large Format and other areas. The final product is produced and delivered to the client.
